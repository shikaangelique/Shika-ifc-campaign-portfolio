Improving Public Analysis of the Minerals Income Investment Fund (MIIF)

Lead Researcher and Author · 2025-2026 · Natural Resource Governance Institute (NRGI)

Commissioned by NRGI and produced through the Centre for Extractives and Development Africa (CEDA), this study examined the legal and economic governance architecture of Ghana's Minerals Income Investment Fund, covering its statutory framework, institutional design, investment strategy, and accountability mechanisms. The research benchmarked MIIF against the Santiago Principles and three international sovereign wealth fund comparators (Norway's GPFG, Chile's ESSF/PRF, and Botswana's Pula Fund), and drew on Ghana's Petroleum Revenue Management Act as a domestic institutional reference. Findings identified structural gaps in disclosure, fiscal rule design, and operational independence, with particular attention to the governance implications of the 2025 legislative amendments that reduced MIIF's statutory share of mineral royalties from 80% to 2%. The study produced evidence-based recommendations across governance, transparency, and mineral revenue management framework design, intended to support civil society and parliamentary advocacy for MIIF reform.
